Distinguishing Potential Misconfiguration from Exploitability

In modern application security, missing an X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors header flags a defensive gap, but does not guarantee immediate exploitability. Real-world clickjacking viability requires a sensitive, state-changing user action, absent or bypassable CSRF/SameSite cookie boundaries, and predictable victim layout.
